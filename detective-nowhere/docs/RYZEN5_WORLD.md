# Ryzen-5 — Planetary Map & Travel Network

Built from the Ryzen-5 atlas (`public/world/ryzen5_atlas.jpg`). Data: `content/world.json` (generated by `tools/build_world.py`). Math: `src/world/planet.ts`. In game: **HoloBand → MAP → PLANET** (2D map, layers, route planner, 3D globe).

## How the scale works
- The scale bar reads **1000 km = 166 px → 6.0 km/px**.
- Atlas height × 6.0 km = **6,144 km**. That equals pole-to-pole on a 3,912 km-diameter planet (½ × 12,289 km circumference).
- Atlas width × 6.0 km = **9,216 km**. That equals **270° of longitude**.

So the atlas is an **equirectangular world projection** running from the north pole to the south pole. The remaining 90° of longitude is off-map and is treated as unsurveyed ocean. The prime meridian is set through Zixel.

All distances are great-circle distances on the sphere. The scale bar is exact at the equator. Toward the poles the atlas stretches east–west, as every equirectangular map does, so the Ashen Reach and Titan Watch are closer together than they look. Airship lanes are drawn as true great circles, which is why they curve on the flat map.

Checks (in `tests/planet.test.ts`):
- 166 px at the equator measures ≈1,000 km.
- The map height measures 6,145 km.
- No two places on the map are more than 6,145 km apart, matching the atlas travel note.
- Most major-city trips take under 3 sols.

## What's in the data
- **8 cities, 15 listed outposts (+4 unnamed duplicate markers), 2 hostile hives.** Positions are measured from the atlas. Population and area come from the atlas panels.
- **26 connection points and roads.** Major roads were traced from the red dotted lines. They are marked `traced`, or `inferred` where the dots are hidden by icons or labels.
- **Sea lanes and airship routes.** These are *proposed*: the atlas doesn't draw them. Kitsah and Wayfarer's Rest are the atlas "Transit Hubs", so they anchor the airship network.
- **Trade corridors:** Meridian Trunk, Highland Road, Titanspine North, Western Frontier, Ironwood Road, Ardent Road, the Equatorial Sea Lane and the Transit Airship Network. Corridor routes come from the atlas roads. The goods listed are suggestions only.
- **Coastlines, lakes and major rivers.** These were segmented from the atlas art into `public/world/ryzen5_landmask.png`, so the HoloBand map traces your coastlines exactly.
- **Speeds (proposed tuning):** road convoy 55 km/h, sea 35 km/h, airship 140 km/h, plus boarding time. A sol is 30 h.

## Open questions — atlas vs. design docs
- Outposts Red Mesa Post (1), Scorpion Watch (2) and Coyote Crossing (8) have no marker on the atlas, while numbers 3, 9 and 12 are printed more than once. Assign names to the duplicate markers.
- An unnamed city icon numbered "3" sits north of Zixel (Port Veyra is city 3 on the west coast).
- A stray "7" label is printed beside Kessar Bay with no outpost triangle.
- Kessar Bay is listed as a coastal city but drawn ~120 km inland on the River Kessar.
- Atlas land covers ~30% of the sphere (~40% of the mapped 270 deg), but the stated surface water is 38% (62% land).
- Western Act V facility city is still TBD; Port Veyra is the only major western city on the atlas.
- Bible lists 5 additional cities and 4 outposts for gameplay; confirmed so far: Kitsah, Kessar Bay, Wayfarer's Rest (+ Port Veyra via side mission S3-01) and Titan Watch.
