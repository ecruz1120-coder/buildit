# Ryzen-5 — Travelable Overworld, Planetary Map & Travel Network

Ryzen-5 is a **playable overworld**: Cedric travels across the whole planet in 3D, at true scale.
- **Overworld:** `src/modes/overworld.ts`. Reach it from the title screen ("Travel Ryzen-5") or HoloBand → MAP → PLANET → "Open the overworld". Entering Zixel from the overworld drops you into the Kaelis Row block.
- **Planetary map and route planner:** HoloBand → MAP → **PLANET**, with a 2D map, layers and a 3D globe.
- **Data:** `content/world.json`, generated by `tools/build_world.py`. Planet math is in `src/world/planet.ts`.
- **Art:** the source atlas is `public/world/ryzen5_atlas.jpg`. The overworld uses `ryzen5_terrain.jpg`, a copy of the atlas with its printed icons, numbers and labels painted out (`tools/terrain_texture.py`), so corrected names never clash with the art. The land/water mask is `ryzen5_landmask.png`.

## Travelling
| | |
|---|---|
| Free travel | WASD / left stick across land. Speed depends on terrain: **road 55 km/h**, **open country 22 km/h**, **mountains 9 km/h** (all proposed tuning). Water blocks you. |
| Time | The world clock advances by true great-circle km ÷ speed. One real second on a road is about 2.2 h of game time. 30 h per sol. |
| Locations | Walk up to a place and press E/A to open its panel. It shows population and coordinates, plus actions: **Enter** (Zixel → Kaelis Row; other scenes aren't built yet), **Airship** / **Ferry** departures with distance and time, **Rest 8 h**, and **Plan a route**. |
| Set course | HoloBand → MAP → PLANET → choose a destination and modes → **Set course**. Cedric then auto-travels each leg: road legs follow the traced roads, then he boards ferries and airships. On road legs, any movement input cancels. |
| Discovery | Cities are known from the start. Outposts, ports, landings and hives appear when Cedric comes within about 170 km. This is knowledge-gated, per the design bible. |
| Terrain | 3D relief built from the atlas mask: coast slopes plus the painted ranges (Titanspine, Razorback, Red Fang, Stormbreaker, the northern massif, the Meridian highlands and the Shattered Expanse). |
| Saving | F5/F9 save and restore your overworld position, odometer and discoveries. |

## Scale
- The scale bar reads **1000 km = 166 px**.
- Atlas height = **6,144 km = pole to pole** (half of the 12,289 km circumference).
- Atlas width = **270° of longitude**.

So the atlas is an equirectangular world projection. Distances are great-circle distances on a 3,912 km sphere, east-west distance shrinks with latitude, and airship lanes are true great circles. The prime meridian runs through Zixel. The off-map 90° is treated as unsurveyed ocean.
